01 / THE PROJECT

More than a finished robot.

Engineering is a cycle of ideas, prototypes, testing, and decisions. The final mechanism is only part of the story.

This portfolio documents contributions to a 2026 FIRST Robotics Competition robot across mechanical design, fabrication, programming, and team leadership. One specific area of involvement was helping program and iterate on the robot's intake.

The sections below describe the engineering workflow and identify where project-specific evidence, design decisions, and test data can be added. Details that have not yet been supplied are marked rather than assumed.

An engineering workflow for documenting the project. Add actual decisions and evidence as they become available.

01
UNDERSTAND THE PROBLEM

Define the challenge.

Start with what the robot needs to accomplish. Identify game-piece geometry, space limitations, available hardware, and the conditions the mechanism must handle. Translate these into testable requirements.

DESIGN + FABRICATE

Turn concepts into hardware.

Explore mechanism layouts, check fit and accessibility, and fabricate parts for assembly. CAD, prototypes, and manufacturing notes can show how constraints affected the design.

03
PROGRAM + INTEGRATE

Make the mechanism respond.

Develop and refine the intake's control behavior, integrate it with the rest of the robot, and check how commands translate into real-world motion. Record the actual code changes and why they were made.

TO ADD: code excerpts, control logic, integration notes

04
TEST + ITERATE

Learn from every run.

Observe what works and what does not, isolate the cause of a problem, make a targeted change, and test again. Compare before-and-after behavior to determine whether the revision helped.
